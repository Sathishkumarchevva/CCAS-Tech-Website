1. Sign in to LinkedIn with a personal profile that will be an admin. A company page cannot exist without at least one personal admin.
2. Click "For Business" (top right, grid icon) → "Create a Company Page" → "Company". If a page already exists, ask the owner to add you as a Super admin instead.
3. Paste the name, public URL, website, industry, size and type from Section 1. Add the logo and tagline. Tick the box confirming you are authorised, then "Create page".
4. Open the page as admin → "Edit page" (or Admin tools → Edit page). Paste the About text, add specialties, location, phone, hashtags and the custom button. Upload the cover banner.
5. Add at least two admins (one can leave or lose access) from Admin tools → Manage admins.
6. Publish Post 1 and pin it, then Posts 2 to 5 over the next two weeks (not all on one day).
7. Invite your team to follow the page and add "CCAS Tech" as their current employer. Share the page link in email signatures and on business cards.
8. Tell us the final LinkedIn URL so it can be added to the website footer (src/data/site.ts → linkedin) and the other website pages.
